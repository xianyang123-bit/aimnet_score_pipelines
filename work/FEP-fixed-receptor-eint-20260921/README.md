# FEP deposited-pose ranking with fixed-receptor AIMNet2 interaction energy

This run ranks the 461 ligands in the 16-system FEP benchmark using the receptor-aligned poses distributed with LigUnity. It reuses the canonical fixed-receptor interaction-energy implementation in `work/eint_pipeline/` and does **not** redock the ligands.

The ligand SDFs contain explicit hydrogens and coordinates aligned with their corresponding protein structures. Their headers and filenames identify them as Schrödinger-prepared, automapped, or manually adjusted congeneric poses. They are suitable bound-pose inputs, but the available files do not establish that every pose was generated by an independent docking calculation.

The correlation is computed within each system between experimental pAffinity and `-Eint`, so larger values indicate better affinity ranking. The final ligand tables sort `Eint` ascending: rank 1 is the most favorable predicted interaction energy.

For context, Boltz-2 has mean Spearman 0.6414 on the same 14 systems and 0.6150 on all 16 systems in `virtual-screening/results/T6_FEP_boltz.csv`. AIMNet2 interaction energy is higher on 4 of the 14 matched systems. These numbers use different scoring models but the same experimental series.

## Protocol

```text
LigUnity FEP SDF poses
        ↓
map the 461 benchmark entries to deposited poses
        ↓
build a residue-labelled 6 Å receptor pocket
        ↓
add one-residue padding and ACE/NME caps
        ↓
add receptor hydrogens at pH 7.4 with Amber14
        ↓
validate complete heavy atoms and integer pocket charge
        ↓
keep every receptor atom fixed
        ↓
relax all ligand atoms with the existing FIRE optimizer
        ↓
Eint = E(complex) - E(pocket) - E(ligand at bound geometry)
        ↓
rank each congeneric series by ascending Eint
```

AIMNet2 uses the `aimnet2_2025_b973c_d3_0` checkpoint. Ligand optimization stops at a maximum force below 0.002 eV/Å or after 1,000 steps. Finite nonconverged results are retained and flagged. The maximum recorded energy-decomposition error is `2.86e-9 kcal/mol`.

### Pose-to-label mapping

Several source SDFs contain extra protomers or mapping variants. Each benchmark ligand was matched using both:

1. Molecular topology after ignoring protonation, aromatic bond representation, and stereochemical annotation.
2. The deposited `r_exp_dg`, which reproduces benchmark pAffinity through `r_exp_dg = -1.364 × pAffinity` within 0.005 kcal/mol.

When the source contained duplicate deposited variants satisfying both checks, the first source record was retained. Every selected ligand preserves `source_index`, `mol_id`, pAffinity, formal charge, and the benchmark SMILES. All 461 benchmark entries mapped successfully.

## Chemistry and coverage decisions

Two systems were excluded rather than assigned an unsupported receptor charge:

- **PFKFB3:** phosphate species `POP` lies 2.47 Å from the pocket and requires an explicit substrate/cofactor protocol.
- **Thrombin:** a sodium ion lies 2.49 Å from the pocket and requires a metal/ion-aware protocol.

BACE contains deposited tartaric acid (`TLA`). It was documented and omitted as a crystallization additive; the resulting capped protein pocket passed the hydrogen, topology, and integer-charge checks.

Seven individual deposited poses were rejected before energy evaluation because their closest protein–ligand heavy-atom distance was below 1.6 Å after receptor preparation:

- CDK2: 3 ligands
- SHP2: 2 ligands
- MCL1: 1 ligand
- SYK: 1 ligand

They were not redocked or replaced. This leaves 403 ranked ligands from 410 attempted ligands in the 14 supported systems.

## Reproduction

The run adapter is `work/eint_pipeline/fep.py`. It imports the existing energy, calculator, pocket-preparation, and geometry modules instead of duplicating them.

```bash
cd /home/xianyang/aimnet2_score_pipelines

PY=/home/xianyang/miniconda3/envs/t3-aimnet/bin/python
DEPS=/home/xianyang/aimnet2_score_pipelines/work/pocket-correction-20260921/deps
OUT=/home/xianyang/aimnet2_score_pipelines/work/FEP-fixed-receptor-eint-new

PYTHONPATH="$DEPS" "$PY" work/eint_pipeline/fep.py init --run "$OUT"

# Run prepare once for each task index 0..15 on CPU workers.
PYTHONPATH="$DEPS" "$PY" work/eint_pipeline/fep.py prepare --run "$OUT" --index 0

# Run score once for each supported task index 0..15 on a CUDA worker.
PYTHONPATH="$DEPS" "$PY" work/eint_pipeline/fep.py score --run "$OUT" --index 0

PYTHONPATH="$DEPS" "$PY" work/eint_pipeline/fep.py aggregate --run "$OUT"
```

The completed run used SLURM arrays; exact job identifiers are recorded in `jobs.json`. Preparation and scoring are restartable at the system level, while initialization requires a new output directory.

## Limitations

- This is interaction energy in a fixed, finite receptor cluster. It omits receptor relaxation, explicit solvent response, entropy, and complete binding free energy.
- Protein protonation uses an Amber pH heuristic rather than target-specific pKa calculations.
- Waters are omitted. Conserved waters may matter for individual systems.
- Duplicate deposited protomer/mapping variants are reduced deterministically to the first matching record.
- Seven nonconverged but finite optimizations are retained; removing them changes the mean correlation from 0.4992 to 0.4976.
- This reconstructs an AIMNet2 interaction-energy workflow from public checkpoints. It is not an official affinity-trained AIMNet2(Score) release.
